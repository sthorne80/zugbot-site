# ZugBot Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 21, 2026**

ZugBot is an independent Discord guild companion, management bot, and embedded Activity operated by **ZugBot Development** ("ZugBot", "we", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information ZugBot handles when you use its Discord features or visit [zugbot.net](https://zugbot.net/).

This policy covers ZugBot, not Discord, Battle.net, Blizzard Entertainment, or other services, whose own policies also apply.

## Information we handle

ZugBot processes information needed to operate the features you or a Discord server chooses to use:

| Category | Information and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Discord identity and membership | User IDs, display names, usernames, avatars, server IDs, membership, roles, and relevant channel and message IDs. These support sign-in, permissions, member selection, and Discord interactions. |
| Game characters and verification | Linked Retail World of Warcraft and WoW: Forever character names, realms or game identifiers, chosen main characters, public profile details, ownership-verification state, and verification and expiration dates. |
| Guild events and groups | Event titles, times and time zones, organizer IDs, attendance and character selections, team roles, group membership, notification preferences, and persistent message references. |
| Server administration | Configured channels and roles, onboarding agreement records, staff action reasons, administrative audit entries, and related status information. |
| Moderation and incident reports | If enabled: warnings, timeouts, kick/ban records, case history, reasons, private staff notes and revisions, reports, reported-member IDs, reporter IDs, optional message links, reviewer assignments, and outcomes. |
| Entertainment | Fun posts, Brewfest keg claims, Peon Fight Club challenges, action choices, dice results, match outcomes, and win/loss statistics, when those features are available. |
| Operations and support | Request IDs, delivery status, timestamps, error and security logs, and information you choose to send when requesting assistance. |

ZugBot may retrieve current Discord member, role, guild, and channel information from Discord rather than permanently storing every detail it displays.

## Discord Activity sign-in

The embedded Activity requests the **identify** and **guilds** Discord OAuth scopes. It uses these to identify your account and verify that you are a member of the server. ZugBot also enforces server-side permissions for protected features. The Activity processes a short-lived Discord access token for authorized API requests; your Discord password is not collected by ZugBot.

Access to the Activity does not mean that a user may read confidential guild records or use staff tools.

## Optional Battle.net verification

Battle.net ownership verification is optional. You can link a public character manually instead. If you choose verification, you sign in on Battle.net's pages, not ZugBot's.

ZugBot does **not collect or store your Battle.net password**. OAuth codes, access tokens, and raw Blizzard account data are processed temporarily rather than retained in the long-term character roster. Stored character records may include Blizzard character identifiers and matching information needed to confirm ownership. Ownership verification is current for **30 days**; expiration does not automatically unlink a character.

See [Battle.net Verification & Privacy](../verification/) for additional technical details.

## When information is visible or shared

- **Members in a Discord channel:** Events, groups, publicly posted character cards, game results, challenges, and other messages can be seen by people with access to the channel where they are posted. Discord controls the platform and server visibility.
- **Authorized guild staff:** Administrative actions, moderation case details, confidential incident reports, and staff notes are intended only for staff who pass ZugBot's role- and permission-based checks. A guild's server administrators govern which staff receive the applicable roles and permissions.
- **Service operation:** ZugBot operators and technical infrastructure providers may need access to records, logs, and backups to operate, secure, investigate, and restore the service.
- **Third-party APIs:** When you request character information or ownership verification, ZugBot communicates with Blizzard's APIs. Discord handles the platform's messages, authorization, and user interactions.

ZugBot does **not sell personal information**. We do not intentionally disclose private guild casework to unrelated guilds.

Please do not put passwords, access tokens, payment information, or other sensitive secrets into Discord commands, incident reports, or support messages.

## Retention, corrections, and deletion

Different records are kept for different operational purposes:

- Linked characters, guild configuration, events, groups, moderation case history, audit records, and Tavern game history may remain stored while required to provide or administer the service.
- The **30-day verification period** expires without automatically erasing character links or their historical records.
- Private staff notes and incident reports are kept while the guild uses ZugBot. The operating policy calls for a review of closed reports after **180 days** and staff notes **annually**. Those reviews are **not an automatic deletion schedule**.
- Case histories, staff-note revisions, and report events are retained for audit accountability. There is currently **no universal self-service deletion feature** for these records.
- Service backups can hold older copies of information until backup rotation or a separate retention/recovery operation is completed.

To request access, a correction, or deletion of information related to your account, contact **[support@zugbot.net](mailto:support@zugbot.net)**. Include your Discord user ID and the relevant server, but **do not send passwords or OAuth tokens**. We may need to verify account ownership, consider legitimate security and audit requirements, and coordinate with a server's authorized administrators. We will assess what can be changed or deleted and explain relevant limitations. Availability of particular rights may depend on applicable law.

You can use ZugBot's supported character-unlinking controls to remove linked characters. Removing ZugBot from a Discord server or allowing verification to expire does not necessarily immediately erase all historical records and backups.

## Website and external links

The public documentation website is hosted using **GitHub Pages**. The hosting platform and infrastructure providers may handle technical connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs, under their own privacy practices. The documentation site does not require a Discord login merely to read these policies.

External links may lead to Discord, Battle.net, Patreon, Buy Me a Coffee, and other third parties. Their collection and payment practices are governed by their policies, not this one.

## Security and eligibility

We use authenticated requests, guild-scoped access controls, permission checks, operational logging, and backups to protect and maintain the service. However, no system offers absolute security.

ZugBot is intended for people eligible to use Discord under its applicable age and account requirements.
