Or keep using the familiar slash commands. ZugBot runs inside Discord.</p><div class="zb-actions"><a class="zb-button zb-button-primary" href="discord-activity/">Read the Activity guide</a><a class="zb-button" href="https://discord.gg/QjZHFW9gwP" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Try ZugBot Live</a><a class="zb-button" href="commands/">Browse commands</a></div></div>
    <div class="zb-operating-notes"><p><strong>Battle.net sign-in is optional and stays on Battle.net.</strong><span>When you choose verification, ZugBot sends your browser to Battle.net's own sign-in page. You can always link characters manually instead.</span></p><p><strong>ZugBot keeps only what it needs.</strong><span>It remembers which characters Battle.net confirmed for your Discord account and when the 30-day confirmation ends. It does not save your Battle.net password or temporary sign-in information.</span></p><p><strong>Your guild stays your guild.</strong><span>Each server keeps its own setup, leadership roles, and activity information.</span></p><p><strong>Ready after a restart.</strong><span>Your active groups and event cards come back after ZugBot restarts.</span></p></div>
